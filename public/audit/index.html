Agent Write-Path Check

Your agent writes to real systems: invoices, emails, orders, tickets. We test whether it duplicates a write after a timeout, and whether it reports success that never happened.

What you get

  • Your write actions tested against 10 failure cases (lost response after timeout, 429, malformed response, contradictory status)
  • A report with findings and run IDs, delivered by a private download link
  • Results from deterministic runs, not from opinions
